# BilimQuest

«Преврати учёбу в приключение!» BilimQuest is a 3D island of knowledge for pupils of grades 5 to 9 and teachers of Kyrgyz schools. Instead of a list of tasks, the pupil walks the island: finds the building of a subject, solves its missions, earns XP, levels and achievements, and asks Robik when a topic is hard. The teacher follows the class in the teacher's room.

A browser prototype made for the children's creative projects contest CREAPARK 2026 «Келечек».
